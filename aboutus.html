Our Story
Every great flavour has a story, and ours begins with a passion for good food. At Flame 95, we believe food is more than just something you eat – it's an experience worth remembering. From delicious bites to mouth-watering meals, every dish is prepared with care, quality ingredients, and a love for flavour. Our goal is simple: to bring people together over great food, create memorable moments, and make every visit special.
What we Sell
At Flame 95, we offer a delicious variety of food to satisfy every craving. Our menu features flavourful kebab parathas, freshly prepared shawarmas, and juicy kebabs, along with a selection of fried rice, including both vegetarian and egg options. We also serve crispy chicken pakoda and delicious chicken popcorn, prepared to deliver a satisfying taste in every bite. Whether you're looking for a quick snack or a filling meal, Flame 95 brings together quality, taste, and variety to make every visit enjoyable.
